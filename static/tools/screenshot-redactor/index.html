Screenshot Redactor

Black out sensitive parts of an image — in your browser

Paste a screenshot (Ctrl+V / ⌘V), drop an image here, or

Blur and pixelation can sometimes be partly reversed or guessed, especially on short text such as numbers. Use a solid box for anything that must stay secret.

Drag on the image to cover an area. On a phone, drag with one finger; use Move to scroll.

Optional. Text recognition runs on your device; the first time it loads about 10 MB of recognition files from this site.

The exported file is a fresh image: redactions are burned into the pixels and no metadata (EXIF, GPS, camera) is copied.
