## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by the websites you visit. This site also uses similar technologies such as `localStorage`, browser storage that stays on your device and is never sent to a server.

## 2. What We Use

### Strictly necessary (no consent required, first-party, never shared)

| Technology | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `localStorage: menu-scroll-position` | Restores the horizontal scroll position of the top menu | Until you clear it |
| `localStorage` keys of individual tools and games (e.g. `easycron-theme`, `nb_best`, `st_state`, high scores) | Remember a tool setting, your best score or a saved game | Until you clear it |
| Service Worker cache | Speeds up repeat visits | Managed by your browser |

### Analytics

**None that use cookies.** Our analytics tool, Umami, is completely cookieless and anonymous. This site does not use Google Analytics.

### Advertising (only with your consent)

Google AdSense and its certified partners may set cookies (for example on `doubleclick.net`) to show and measure ads and, with your consent, to personalize them. Google's consent banner stores your choice in its own cookies (such as `FCCDCF` and `FCNEC`). Details: [How Google uses cookies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies).

### Affiliate partners

This site sets no affiliate cookies. If you click a link labelled "Affiliate link", the partner's website may set its own cookies on its domain to attribute the referral (see the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/#7-affiliate-links)).

## 3. Managing Your Consent

- **Cookie Settings**: use the link in the footer of every page to reopen the consent banner and change or withdraw your choices at any time.
- **Browser controls**: you can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.
- **Google controls**: [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com) for ad personalization.
- **Industry opt-out**: [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/).

## 4. One Important Note

The interactive tools on this site process your data **locally in your browser**. They set no tracking cookies and upload nothing. See the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/) for details, including the one tool (NetGuard) that contacts an IP-lookup service.
