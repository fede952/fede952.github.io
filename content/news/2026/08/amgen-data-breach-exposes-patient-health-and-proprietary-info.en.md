Pharmaceutical giant Amgen confirms a cloud data breach involving third-party providers, exposing patient health and proprietary corporate data.

{{< cyber-report severity="High" source="BleepingComputer" target="Amgen cloud systems" >}}

Amgen, a major pharmaceutical company, has disclosed a data breach that compromised patient health information and proprietary corporate data. The breach affected multiple cloud systems operated by third-party service providers, indicating a supply-chain attack vector.

{{< ad-banner >}}

The stolen data includes sensitive patient health information and proprietary corporate data, which could have significant regulatory and competitive implications. The exact scope of the breach and the number of affected individuals have not been disclosed.

This incident highlights the growing risk of cloud-based data storage and the importance of robust third-party risk management. Organizations must ensure that their cloud providers adhere to strict security standards and that data is protected with encryption and access controls.
