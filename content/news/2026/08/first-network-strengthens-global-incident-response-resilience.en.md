With interconnected supply chains, no organization can handle incidents alone. FIRST connects over 800 IR teams, as shown by Campania's CERT.

In an era of global threats and interconnected supply chains, cybersecurity incidents cannot be managed in isolation. The Forum of Incident Response and Security Teams (FIRST) addresses this by connecting over 800 incident response teams worldwide, fostering a trusted ecosystem for sharing threat intelligence and best practices.

The article highlights the journey of the CERT of the Campania Region in Italy, illustrating how regional teams can leverage such networks to enhance their incident response capabilities. By participating in FIRST, even smaller organizations gain access to a global community, enabling faster and more effective responses to cyber threats.

This collaborative approach is essential for building cyber resilience, as it allows organizations to learn from each other's experiences and coordinate during large-scale incidents. The emphasis is on proactive preparation and the power of community over isolated efforts.
