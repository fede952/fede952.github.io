{{< cyber-report severity="Medium" source="BleepingComputer" target="Email users from breached databases" >}}

The campaign relies on the credibility of the leaked data to pressure victims. While no technical exploitation is involved, the social engineering aspect poses a significant risk to individuals whose credentials were compromised.

Organizations should advise users to ignore such demands and report them. Enabling multi-factor authentication and monitoring for credential stuffing attacks can mitigate broader impacts from the underlying breaches.
