A collection of interactive tools for developers, sysadmins and security professionals. They run in your browser: the files and text you work on stay on your device. Two tools contact an outside service, and only for the feature that needs it — NetGuard looks up your public IP address with ipwho.is, and PassFort's optional breach check sends 5 characters of a password hash to Have I Been Pwned when you press its button.
