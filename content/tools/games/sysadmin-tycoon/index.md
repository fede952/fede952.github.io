Start with a humble laptop and build a server empire. Balance power consumption, cooling, and cash flow while surviving DDOS attacks, power surges, and other IT disasters.

SysAdmin Tycoon auto-saves your progress and calculates offline earnings (up to 8 hours).

## How to Play

- Buy machines in the **Servers** tab: each one earns money every second (**Income/s**) and needs power and cooling.
- Keep the **Power** bar below its limit with the **Power** tab (extension cords, UPS, generators…): above the limit the room has a blackout and stops earning.
- Keep the **Heat** bar below its limit with the **Cooling** tab: above it the room overheats and income is halved.
- **Upgrades** cut server costs or raise income permanently.
- Random events: a **DDOS attack** asks you to click the defence button 20 times within 10 seconds; a **power surge** raises consumption by 50% for 15 seconds.
- **Sell mode** lets you click a machine in the room to sell it. Progress is saved in your browser, with offline earnings for up to 8 hours.
